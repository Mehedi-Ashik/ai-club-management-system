\section{Project Overview}
%% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The \textbf{AI-Powered University Club Management System} is a full-scale, publicly accessible web platform for managing university club operations. Unlike a simple internal tool, this system has a \textbf{public-facing website} where guests can browse events and club information, and a \textbf{secure member portal} for registered users with role-based access.

\begin{infobox}{accent}{Platform Architecture}
\textbf{Two distinct layers:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Public Layer} --- Landing page, event listings, blog, contact, online event registration \& payment
  \item \textbf{Private Layer} --- Member portal, admin dashboard, attendance, certificates, forum, analytics
\end{itemize}
\end{infobox}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{notebox}
\textbf{What makes this project stand out:} Real payment integration (SSLCommerz/bKash), public blog \& forum, AI content generation, automated certificate delivery, and a fully deployed live URL.
\end{notebox}

\section{Project Objectives}
%% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=5pt]
  \item \textbf{Build a public website} with landing page, about, blog, and contact sections
  \item \textbf{Enable online event registration} for both members and guests
  \item \textbf{Integrate payment gateway} for event fees and membership dues
  \item \textbf{Manage club members} with advanced role-based access control
  \item \textbf{Automate attendance} tracking with QR code support
  \item \textbf{Generate and email certificates} automatically post-event
  \item \textbf{Provide a blog \& news} platform managed by admin/president
  \item \textbf{Host a discussion forum} for club members
  \item \textbf{Deliver real-time analytics} through an advanced dashboard
  \item \textbf{Integrate AI features} for event descriptions and blog drafts
\end{enumerate}

\section{Database Design Overview}
%% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The database is designed in \textbf{Third Normal Form (3NF)} with the following core tables:

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.45}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries\ttfamily\small}l X}
\toprule
\textbf{Table} & \textbf{Key Attributes} \\
\midrule
User           & id, username, email, password\_hash, role, is\_active, created\_at \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Member         & id, user\_id (FK), full\_name, dept, batch, roll, phone, avatar \\
Event          & id, title, description, category, date, venue, capacity, fee, status, is\_public \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Registration   & id, user\_id (FK), event\_id (FK), reg\_type, registered\_at \\
Attendance     & id, member\_id (FK), event\_id (FK), status, marked\_at \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Payment        & id, user\_id (FK), event\_id (FK), amount, method, txn\_id, status \\
Certificate    & id, member\_id (FK), event\_id (FK), cert\_id (unique), file\_path, issued\_at \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
BlogPost       & id, author\_id (FK), title, content, category, status, published\_at \\
Comment        & id, post\_id (FK), user\_id (FK), body, created\_at \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
ForumThread    & id, author\_id (FK), title, category, is\_pinned, created\_at \\
ForumReply     & id, thread\_id (FK), author\_id (FK), body, upvotes, created\_at \\
\rowcolor{rowgray}
Notification   & id, user\_id (FK), message, type, is\_read, created\_at \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
